Who I Am

Building at the intersection of AI and design

I'm Kanishk Bhardwaj, an AI Developer and Researcher based in Haridwar, Uttarakhand, India. I specialize in building intelligent systems that are not just functional — but beautiful, interactive, and memorable.

I also coined the term "Vibe Coder" — a developer who merges technical excellence with creative design instinct. My book "Vibe To Code", published on Amazon Kindle in March 2026, is a comprehensive guide to this mindset.

My research has been published at the International Conference on Automation for Sustainable Future (March 2025), focusing on AI-powered crop recommendation using soil understanding.
